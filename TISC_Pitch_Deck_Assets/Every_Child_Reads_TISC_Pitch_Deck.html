One Teacher. Many Students. Every Reading Difficulty Matters.

The Real Classroom Challenge of Understanding Every Child’s Oral Reading Needs

SHR TRINITY AksharaMitra
CLASSROOM REALITY • 1 TEACHER : 35+ STUDENTS
Why Individual Reading Difficulties Go Unseen

In a typical school classroom of 35+ students across Classes 1 to 10, listening to each child read aloud and manually recording every pronunciation or fluency hurdle is a daily time bottleneck.

01 • TIME BOTTLENECK
Limited Individual Assessment Time
Teachers have only minutes per student, limiting how frequently each child’s oral reading can be evaluated.
02 • HIDDEN ERRORS
Pronunciation Mistakes Go Unnoticed
Subtle articulation errors and silent decoding struggles are easily missed during group reading sessions.
03 • MIXED PROFICIENCY
Different Abilities in One Classroom
Children within the same grade read at widely different proficiency levels yet receive identical practice texts.
04 • MANUAL LOGGING LIMITS
Hard to Track Skipped & Repeated Words
Manually recording skipped words, repeated words, hesitation pauses, and reading speed in real time is impractical.
05 • DIAGNOSTIC GAP
Lack of Detailed Bilingual Diagnostics
Teachers lack granular insight into specific Telugu Ottulu / Mahapranalu or English phonics difficulties per child.
06 • DELAYED REMEDIATION
Delayed Personalized Support
Without early diagnostic visibility, struggling readers do not receive targeted practice when they need it most.

1. TELUGU OTTULU & CONJUNCT CONSONANTS సంయుక్తాక్షరాలు
Telugu Syllable Segmentation Practice
• Identified Difficulty: Simplifying compound clusters in words like “సస్యశ్యామలంగా” or “ప్రవాహం”.
• Real Practice Example: Step-by-step syllable breakdown (స - స్య - శ్యా - మ - లం - గా and ప్ర - వా - హం) paired with graduated Ottulu word ladders.
Proposed Practice Output: Builds automatic recognition of Telugu conjunct clusters.
2. TELUGU MAHAPRANALU మహాప్రాణాక్షరాలు
Alpaprana & Mahaprana Contrast Drills
• Identified Difficulty: Substituting unaspirated consonants for aspirated ఖ, ఘ, ఛ, థ, ధ, ఫ, భ.
• Real Practice Example: Side-by-side Alpaprana vs. Mahaprana contrast pairs (e.g., దైర్యం vs. ధైర్యం, ఫలం vs. పలం, మేఘం) with guided articulation practice.
Proposed Practice Output: Strengthens clear aspirated consonant pronunciation.
3. ENGLISH PHONICS & PRONUNCIATION Syllable Chunking
English Syllable Chunking & Phonics Families
• Identified Difficulty: Struggling with multi-syllabic words or vowel blends (e.g., “environment”, “curious”).
• Real Practice Example: Visual syllable chunking (en - vi - ron - ment, cu - ri - ous) and rhyming phonics word-family drills.
Proposed Practice Output: Improves multi-syllabic decoding and word stress.
4. READING FLUENCY, PACING & CONFIDENCE Guided Reading
Short Guided Reading & Rhythm Exercises
• Identified Difficulty: Frequent mid-sentence hesitation pauses, word repetitions, or skipped connectors.
• Real Practice Example: Short phrase-grouped guided reading passages and repeated-reading confidence builders matched to the child’s level.
Proposed Practice Output: Builds smooth pacing, cadence, and reading confidence.
